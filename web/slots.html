PAYTABLE
How it works: 3 reels spin. The win line is the middle row. Get 3 of the same symbol on the win line to win (bet × multiplier). The star (WILD) stands in for any symbol. Each spin has a fixed chance of each symbol. The expected return is about 92.6% of your bets, so over time you get back a bit less than you bet.
